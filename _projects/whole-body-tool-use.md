Humans are incredibly good at leveraging their entire body when interacting with the world and tools around them: opening doors with a foot while holding a tray or package, using an elbow to push a button when their hands are occupied, or pulling a chair over with a foot. This project asks: what does it take for a robot to unlock the same capabilities?
