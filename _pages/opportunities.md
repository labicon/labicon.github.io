## Join the <u>I</u>ntelligent <u>Con</u>trol Lab

Thank you for your interest in our research! We are actively seeking motivated new talent and strongly encourage students from diverse backgrounds to apply. Due to the high volume of inquiries we receive, we are unable to respond to every message individually, but we appreciate your interest.

Please review the following guidelines based on your current status:

### Current UC Berkeley Undergraduates

If you are a current UC Berkeley undergraduate interested in joining the lab, please review our [active projects]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}) and identify those that best match your interests. Then complete the [application form](https://forms.gle/mnma3Y8DgmGoNPW36). You may send a brief follow-up email to [iconresearchlab@gmail.com](mailto:iconresearchlab@gmail.com) and CC a graduate student whose work interests you. We review applications on a rolling basis, especially near the beginning of each semester, and invite selected applicants to interview in person.

### Prospective Graduate Students

Please apply through the formal UC Berkeley graduate admissions process. We look for self-motivated students with strong foundations in robotics, control theory, optimization, computer vision, safe reinforcement learning, and machine learning. You do not need to contact us directly at this stage; instead, please highlight your interest in our lab within your Statement of Purpose.

### Postdoctoral Applicants, Visiting Scholars, & Research Interns

We are currently updating our application system, and the specific forms for these roles will be posted here soon. In the meantime, please <i>email the PI, Negar Mehr, directly</i> with your CV, a cover letter, and a list of 3–5 references (or your research proposal for visiting positions) to express your interest.
