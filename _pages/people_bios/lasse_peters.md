Lasse is a postdoctoral scholar in the Mechanical Engineering department at UC Berkeley. He develops algorithms that let robots handle complex physical and social interaction, from contact-rich manipulation and agile flight to anticipating the behavior of surrounding humans and robots. His work combines reinforcement learning, imitation learning, and dynamic game theory to learn policies and interaction models from limited data and to keep robots safe under model uncertainty, with validation on manipulators, mobile robots, and quadrotors. Prior to Berkeley, he completed his PhD at the Department of Cognitive Robotics at Delft University of Technology, where he wrote his dissertation on "Game-Theoretic Motion Planning for Multi-Agent Interaction".
