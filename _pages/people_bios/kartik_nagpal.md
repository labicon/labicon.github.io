Kartik is a PhD Candidate in the Mechanical Engineering Department at the University of California at Berkeley. His research interests revolve around learning intricate multi-robot behavior for safety-critical environments, with an emphasis on safe reinforcement learning and multiagent coordination. Kartik earned his B.S. in Computational Engineering from the University of Texas at Austin, during which he did research with Dr. Ufuk Topcu and Dr. Moriba Jah. Kartik is funded by the NSF Graduate Research Fellowship.
